Coming Through!

Get from the blue pad to the green pad without bumping into anyone.

Touch and drag anywhere to move. Keyboard: WASD or arrows, shift to hurry. C cycles the camera, O toggles ink, [ and ] change the crowd size, T cycles the device tier, G toggles the debug view. R replays the current seed from the start, N rolls a new one.

Same seed, same crowd, every time. Share the link to race a friend through the same people.
